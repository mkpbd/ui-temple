Our Story

Founded in 2010, Lumina began with a simple belief: that the environment we inhabit profoundly shapes our well-being. What started as a small boutique studio has grown into a multidisciplinary collective of designers, architects, and artists.

We reject the temporary. Our designs are rooted in timeless principles of proportion, light, and materiality. We don't just fill rooms with furniture; we sculpt space to create moments of calm and connection.
